### Narrative & Thematic Summary
Episode 15 marks an emotional turning point in the series, framed by the suffocating, humid backdrop of the Japanese rainy season (梅雨, *tsuyu*). The core emotional arc revolves around the painful collisions between romantic feelings and friendship:
1. **Kuriki Momoka's Calculated Boldness:** Underclassman Momoka observes Minato's psychological defense mechanism—playing dumb and remaining passive to avoid getting hurt—and decides to push into his life proactively by inviting him to a band live show.
2. **Miki's Guilt and Agony:** Having rejected Youta's confession, Miki is devastated by the fear that she was thoughtless (無神経) and self-absorbed. She confides in Minato at the juice counter, questioning whether she can ever have a genuine relationship or if she only clings to people who like her.
3. **The Restaurant Booth Overhearing:** Koyuki and Miki sit in a family restaurant booth, while Minato and Youta unknowingly sit right in the booth behind them. Youta confronts Minato about leading girls on with his ambiguous friendliness (気を持たせる). When Youta asks Minato if he could ever have special romantic feelings for Koyuki, Minato—trapped by his fear of ruining their bond—declares: 「友達として ちゃんと大好き」(*I really love her—as a friend*).
4. **Koyuki's Heartbreak & Resignation:** Overhearing Minato's words crushes Koyuki, who was just beginning to realize her romantic feelings for him. She resolves to force her feelings back to "square one" (振り出しに戻す), convincing herself that being valued as a friend is more than enough for someone like her.
5. **Minato's True Inner Despair:** The episode concludes with Minato's private monologue: his words were a protective lie, and his inability to be honest now feels like a cruel joke (タチの悪い冗談) that may permanently prevent Koyuki from ever looking at him romantically.

### Major Characters & Speaking Styles
- **氷川 小雪 (Hikawa Koyuki / こゆん):** Guarded, introverted, highly empathetic towards others while harsh on herself. Speaks in polite, measured tones in public, but reveals deep emotional vulnerability and self-doubt in her internal monologues.
- **雨宮 湊 (Amamiya Minato):** Affable, easygoing high schooler who uses a casual, gentle persona (`行こっか`, `ありがと`, `気にすんな`) to mask severe emotional detachment and fear of rejection.
- **安曇 美姫 (Azumi Miki):** Expressive, bubbly, but currently racked with guilt. Uses emotional, fast-paced colloquial Japanese (`～じゃん？`, `～でくんない？`, `ヤバい`, `吐きそう`).
- **日野 陽太 (Hino Youta):** Sincere, athletic, stoic. Speaks in direct, honest boyish Japanese (`～わ～`, `～かなぁ`, `～んだよね`). Bottles up pain rather than crying.
- **栗木 桃香 (Kuriki Momoka):** Perceptive, assertive underclassman. Uses polite yet sharp conversational Japanese (`～ですもんね`, `グイグイいってる`).
- **緑川 大地 (Midorikawa Daichi):** Minato's close friend; speaks natural **Kansai dialect** (`～へんくて`, `～ええのに`, `～見いひんか`).

### Major Linguistic Challenges
1. **Casual Contractions & Slang:** `～とく` (誘っとく), `～ちゃう/じゃう` (かけちゃいました, 終わっちゃった), `～じゃん` (付き合うじゃん, 断んじゃん), `ディス` (dissing).
2. **Kansai Dialect Features:** `相手にされへんくて` (相手にされなくて), `泣き見いひんか` (泣きを見ないか), `やろうなぁ` (だろうなぁ).
3. **High School Idioms of Romantic Ambiguity:** `気を持たせる` (to lead on), `受け身で待ち` (passive waiting), `振り出しに戻す` (back to square one), `タチの悪い冗談` (nasty joke).

### D. Similar and Easily Confused Vocabulary

#### 1. 避ける (さける) vs かわす (かわす)
- **避ける (さける):** To avoid, steer clear of, or keep a distance from someone or something (physical or emotional).
  - *Example:* 「廊下で目を合わせて避ける」(To look away and avoid someone in the hallway).
- **かわす (かわす):** To dodge, parry, or deflect an active approach, question, or pushy advance.
  - *Example:* 「告白やデートの誘いをうまくかわす」(To deftly deflect a confession or invitation).
  - *In Episode:* Momoka complains in Sub 223: `かわされまくってます` (*He's deflecting all my moves left and right*).

#### 2. 言い訳 (いいわけ) vs 理由 (りゆう)
- **理由 (りゆう):** An objective reason or rationale. Neutral connotation.
- **言い訳 (いいわけ):** An excuse or justification made to defend oneself, escape blame, or avoid taking action. Negative nuance.
  - *In Episode:* Momoka observes in Sub 7: `言い訳を並べて` (*Listing excuse after excuse to justify inaction*).

#### 3. 好意 (こうい) vs 恋愛感情 (れんあいかんじょう)
- **好意 (こうい):** Goodwill, favor, kindness, or general friendly affection. It can be purely platonic or courteous.
- **恋愛感情 (れんあいかんじょう):** Specifically romantic feelings or desire for a romantic relationship.
  - *In Episode:* Koyuki confuses Minato's universal `好意` (goodwill toward everyone) with `恋愛感情` directed uniquely at her.

## 3. Kanji Revision and Learning

### 拒 — Repel / Refuse
- **On'yomi:** キョ
- **Kun'yomi:** こば・む
- **Core Meaning:** To push back, reject, or refuse entry.
- **Episode Vocabulary:** 拒絶 (きょぜつ — rejection / rebuff).
- **Related Vocabulary:** 拒否 (きょひ — refusal/veto), 拒む (こばむ — to decline/refuse).

### 絶 — Sever / Extinguish / Absolute
- **On'yomi:** ゼツ
- **Kun'yomi:** た・える, た・やす, た・つ
- **Core Meaning:** To cut off, sever completely, or reach an extreme limit.
- **Episode Vocabulary:** 拒絶 (きょぜつ — rejection), 絶対に (ぜったいに — unconditionally/never).
- **Related Vocabulary:** 絶望 (ぜつぼう — despair), 断絶 (だんぜつ — rupture/breakdown).

### 訳 — Reason / Translation / Circumstance
- **On'yomi:** ヤク
- **Kun'yomi:** わけ

- **Related Vocabulary:** 申し訳ない (もうしわけない — inexcusable/very sorry), 通訳 (つうやく — interpretation).

### 悟 — Realize / Comprehend / Attain Enlightenment
- **On'yomi:** ゴ
- **Kun'yomi:** さと・る
- **Core Meaning:** To awaken mentally to a deep reality; perception.
- **Episode Vocabulary:** 覚悟 (かくご — mental readiness / resolution).
- **Related Vocabulary:** 悟り (さとり — spiritual enlightenment), 覚る (さとる — to discern/comprehend).

### 寂 — Lonely / Still / Desolate
- **On'yomi:** ジャク, セキ
- **Kun'yomi:** さび・しい, さび・れる

- **Episode Vocabulary:** 寂しい (さびしい — lonely), 寂しさ (さびしさ — loneliness).
- **Related Vocabulary:** 静寂 (せいじゃく — dead silence), 寂寥 (せきりょう — loneliness).

### 満 — Full / Fulfill / Satisfy
- **On'yomi:** マン
- **Kun'yomi:** み・ちる, み・たす
- **Core Meaning:** Filled to capacity, satisfied, complete.
- **Episode Vocabulary:** 満たされる (みたされる — to be emotionally fulfilled/satisfied).
- **Related Vocabulary:** 満足 (まんぞく — satisfaction), 満員 (まんいん — full house).

## 4. Compound Word Analysis

### 1. 恋愛対象 (れんあいたいしょう) — Romantic Interest / Prospect
- **恋 (れん):** Romantic yearning / passionate attachment.
- **愛 (あい):** Cherishing love / deep affection.
- **対 (たい):** Facing / opposing / paired.
- **象 (しょう):** Shape / image / phenomenon.
- **Actual Lexical Meaning:** The object/target (`対象`) toward which one directs romantic love (`恋愛`). In teenage Japanese, separates friends from dating prospects.

### 2. 手っ取り早い (てっとりばやい) — Quick and Direct
- **手 (て):** Hand.
- **取り (とり):** Taking / grasping.
- **早い (はやい):** Fast.
- **Actual Lexical Meaning:** Something that can be taken in hand and resolved immediately without circuitous or formal procedures.

### 3. 板挟み (いたばさみ) — Caught in the Middle / In a Bind
- **板 (いた):** Wooden plank / board.
- **挟み (はさみ):** Pinched between / sandwiched.
- **Actual Lexical Meaning:** The psychological distress of being squeezed between two conflicting parties or loyalties (e.g. Minato knowing both Miki's and Youta's private confessions).

### 4. 振り出し (ふりだし) — Square One / Starting Point
- **振る (ふる):** To shake / cast (dice).
- **出す (だす):** To set out / emerge.
- **Actual Lexical Meaning:** Originates from Japanese traditional board games (双六, *sugoroku*), where the starting square is called *furidashi* (where you roll the dice to start). Returning to *furidashi* means resetting back to square one.

## 5. Grammar Required for the Episode

### G-001: 〜わけではない / 〜わけでもない (N3 Core)
- **Meaning:** It does not necessarily mean that...; it is not the case that...
- **Formation:** Verb [Plain Form] + わけではない / い-Adj + わけではない / な-Adj + な + わけではない / Noun + というわけではない
- **Usage Nuance:** Used to partially qualify or refute a total assumption or hasty conclusion without asserting the extreme opposite.
- **Contrast:** Unlike `〜ない` (direct negative assertion) or `〜わけがない` (strong conviction that something is impossible), `〜わけではない` softens a negation.
- **Episode Example:** Sub 46: 「気持ちに応えられるわけでもないのに」(*Even though it's not like I can return his feelings...*).
- **Additional Example:** 嫌いなわけではないが、今は付き合えない。(*It's not that I dislike you, but I can't date right now.*)

### G-002: 〜ように (N3 Core — Purpose)
- **Meaning:** In order to...; so as to...; so that...
- **Formation:** Verb [Non-volitional / Potential / Negative Plain] + ように
- **Usage Nuance:** Focuses on bringing about a state or preventing an adverse result.
- **Episode Example:** Sub 9: 「傷つかないように 自分を守ることで」(*By protecting himself so as not to get hurt...*).
- **Additional Example:** 忘れないようにメモを取っておく。(*I take notes so that I won't forget.*)

### G-003: 〜ておく → 〜とく (N3 Casual Contraction)
- **Meaning:** To do something in advance for future convenience; leave in a certain state.
- **Formation:** Verb [-te form minus `te`] + とく (e.g. 誘っておく → 誘っとく).
- **Usage Nuance:** Ubiquitous in casual daily speech. Indicates proactive preparation.
- **Episode Example:** Sub 26: 「他に来られそうな人も誘っとく」(*I'll invite other people who might be free too.*).

### G-004: 〜てしまう → 〜ちゃう / 〜じゃう (N4/N3 Casual Contraction)
- **Meaning:** Expresses completion, often accompanied by regret, helplessness, or unintended action.
- **Formation:** `〜てしまう` → `〜ちゃう`; `〜でしまう` → `〜じゃう`.
- **Episode Example:** Sub 32: 「通話かけちゃいました」(*I ended up calling you.*); Sub 115: 「終わっちゃったわ～」(*It's totally ended...*).

### G-005: 〜じゃん (Colloquial Pragmatic Particle)
- **Meaning:** Isn't it?; You know that...; Surely...
- **Formation:** Plain form / Noun + じゃん (contraction of ではないか / じゃないか).
- **Usage Nuance:** Seeks agreement, confirms shared background knowledge, or makes a gentle assertion.
- **Episode Example:** Sub 77: 「好きじゃない人とでも気軽に付き合うじゃん？」(*You'll casually go out with someone you don't even like, won't you?*).

### G-006: 〜っけ (N3 Casual Recollection)
- **Meaning:** Was it...?; Did we...?; Remind me, is it...?
- **Formation:** Verb [Past/Ta-form] + っけ / Noun/な-Adj + だ・だった + っけ
- **Usage Nuance:** Asking oneself or a friend to verify a half-forgotten fact.
- **Episode Example:** Sub 53: 「えっ？ 今日だっけ？ ヤバ！」(*Wait, is it today?! Oh crap!*).

### G-007: 〜てくれるな → 〜てくんない？ (Colloquial Request)
- **Meaning:** Won't you please...?; Could you not...?
- **Formation:** Verb [-te form] + くんない？ (contraction of くれない？).
- **Usage Nuance:** Casual request among peers, often carrying mild irritation or exasperation.
- **Episode Example:** Sub 57: 「簡単に言わないでくんない？」(*Could you not say it so casually?*).

### G-008: Kansai Negative `〜へん` & Potential Negative `〜されへん` (Exposure)
- **Meaning:** Does not / Cannot (Kansai dialect equivalent of Standard Japanese `〜ない` and `〜られない`).
- **Formation:** Verb stem + へん (e.g. されない → されへん).
- **Episode Example:** Sub 98: 「まったく相手にされへんくて」(= まったく相手にされなくて, *being completely paid no attention to*).

### G-009: 〜ばええのに (Kansai Dialect Wish / Resentful Hope)
- **Meaning:** It would be good if...; I wish that... (Standard Japanese: 〜ばいいのに).
- **Formation:** Verb Conditional `ば` + ええのに.
- **Episode Example:** Sub 99: 「感情グッチャグチャになればええのに」(*I wish your emotions would get completely wrecked*).

### G-010: 〜ってこと (N3 Conversational Structure)
- **Meaning:** Does that mean that...?; In other words...
- **Formation:** Sentence + ってこと (contraction of ということ).
- **Usage Nuance:** Paraphrases or seeks confirmation of what the other speaker implies.
- **Episode Example:** Sub 109: 「断るか迷ってるってこと？」(*Meaning you're torn about whether to reject him?*).

### G-011: 〜てほしかった (N4/N3 Core)
- **Meaning:** Wanted someone else to do...
- **Formation:** Verb [-te form] + ほしかった.
- **Usage Nuance:** Expresses a past unfulfilled desire regarding another person's actions.
- **Episode Example:** Sub 179: 「ヨータにずっと友達として接してほしかった」(*I wanted Youta to keep treating me just as a friend*).

### G-012: 〜ものだ / 〜もんだ (N3 Core — General Truth / Inevitability)
- **Meaning:** It is natural that...; That's simply the nature of...
- **Formation:** Verb [Plain present] + ものだ / もんだ
- **Usage Nuance:** Explains how human psychology or the world inherently works.
- **Episode Example:** Sub 199: 「勝手になっちゃうもんだし」(*It's the kind of thing that just happens on its own*).

### G-013: 〜まくる (N2/N3 Colloquial Emphasis)
- **Meaning:** To do relentlessly / over and over again / intensely.
- **Formation:** Verb stem + まくる.
- **Episode Example:** Sub 223: 「かわされまくってます」(*I'm being deflected left and right*).

### G-014: 〜おきながら (N2 Contrastive / Reproach)
- **Meaning:** Despite doing...; While having done... (contradiction with reproachful tone).
- **Formation:** Verb [-te form] + おきながら.
- **Episode Example:** Sub 491: 「自分から人を拒絶しておきながら」(*Even though I was the one who rejected others first...*).

### G-015: 〜にしか〜ない (N3 Exclusionary Emphasis)
- **Meaning:** Only; nothing other than; nothing but.
- **Formation:** Noun + にしか + Verb [Negative].
- **Episode Example:** Sub 516: 「タチの悪い冗談にしか なってなくて」(*Has become nothing more than a sick joke*).

### G-016: 〜得る (〜うる / 〜える) (N2 Formal Potential)
- **Meaning:** Can occur; is possible; conceivable.
- **Formation:** Verb stem + 得る (うる / える). Negative: 得ない (えない).
- **Episode Example:** Sub 518: 「あり得るのだろうか」(*Could such a thing even be possible?*).

## 7. Conversational Cues and Listening Patterns

### 1. Colloquial Contractions in Fast Dialogue

- `さあ`: Used by Minato (`美姫さぁ`, Sub 70) and Youta (`ミナトさあ`, Sub 276) to casually cushion a sensitive confrontation.
